Classes:
tw:Country (a kind of tw:Place): A sovereign state.
tw:Customer: A person who has opened an account with Tarnwick. Whether a customer counts as active depends on whose definition is used.
tw:NameMatch: A record that a name typed in one file was judged to mean a known organization, with the score and the method used, so the decision can be checked later.
tw:Order: One purchase by one customer at one store on one day, made up of one or more order lines.
tw:OrderLine: One product within an order, with the quantity bought and the price paid for each unit.
tw:Organization: A company or other body that can own things, sign agreements and be responsible for products.
tw:Place: A named area on the map, from a town up to a country.
tw:Policy: A written Tarnwick rule document, such as the returns policy, in one numbered version.
tw:Product: Something Tarnwick sells, identified by one SKU. Each size or version with its own SKU is a separate product.
tw:ProductStatus: Whether Tarnwick is currently selling a product.
tw:Recall: A public notice that a product is unsafe or faulty and that customers should return it.
tw:Region (a kind of tw:Place): A group of states that Tarnwick reports sales for as one area.
tw:ReturnRule: One rule from a policy saying how long customers have to return products in a category.
tw:State (a kind of tw:Place): A US state.
tw:StockLevel: How many units of one product one store held when the stock was last counted.
tw:Store: A place where Tarnwick sells to customers, either a shop with an address or the online store.
tw:Supplier (a kind of tw:Organization): An organization that Tarnwick buys products from, or has bought products from in the past.
tw:SupportTicket: A message from a customer to Tarnwick's support team, kept as the customer wrote it.
tw:Town (a kind of tw:Place): A town or city.

Properties (from -> to):
tw:aboutOrder (tw:SupportTicket -> tw:Order): Links a support ticket to the order the customer was asking about.
tw:accountClosed (tw:Customer -> xsd:date): The date a customer closed their account. Absent while the account is open.
tw:accountOpened (tw:Customer -> xsd:date): The date a customer's account was created.
tw:announced (tw:Recall -> xsd:date): The date a recall was made public.
tw:appliesToCategory (tw:ReturnRule -> skos:Concept): Links a return rule to the product category it covers, including every category beneath it.
tw:atStore (tw:StockLevel -> tw:Store): Links a stock level to the store that holds the stock.
tw:complaintAbout (tw:SupportTicket -> tw:Product) [also implies tw:mentions]: Links a support ticket to the product the customer reports a problem with. Every product a ticket complains about is also one it mentions, but a ticket can mention other products too.
tw:condition (tw:ReturnRule -> xsd:string): What must be true of a returned product for a rule to apply, in plain words.
tw:country (tw:Organization -> xsd:string): The country an organization is registered in, by its English name.
tw:definedIn (tw:ReturnRule -> tw:Policy): Links a return rule to the policy document that states it.
tw:effective (tw:Policy -> xsd:date): The date from which a policy version applies.
tw:firstSupplied (tw:Supplier -> xsd:date): The date of the first delivery from a supplier.
tw:fits (tw:Product -> tw:Product): Links an accessory to a product it is made to be used with, such as a footprint to the tent it is cut for.
tw:handledUnder (tw:Recall -> tw:Policy): Links a recall to the policy that says how customers are treated.
tw:hasLine (tw:Order -> tw:OrderLine) [inverse of tw:lineOf]: Links an order to one of its order lines.
tw:hasStatus (tw:Product -> tw:ProductStatus) [one value at most]: Links a product to whether it is on sale. A product has exactly one status at a time.
tw:homeTown (tw:Customer -> tw:Town): Links a customer to the town on their account.
tw:inCategory (tw:Product -> skos:Concept): Links a product to a category in the Tarnwick product taxonomy. A product can be in more than one category.
tw:lastSupplied (tw:Supplier -> xsd:date): The date Tarnwick stopped buying from a supplier. Absent for suppliers Tarnwick still buys from.
tw:lineOf (tw:OrderLine -> tw:Order) [inverse of tw:hasLine]: Links an order line to the order it belongs to.
tw:locatedIn (tw:Store -> tw:Town): Links a store to the town it is in. The online store has no location.
tw:mainMaterial (tw:Product -> xsd:string): The material that makes up most of a product, as written on its label.
tw:marketingOptIn (tw:Customer -> xsd:boolean): Whether a customer has agreed to receive marketing email.
tw:matchMethod (tw:NameMatch -> xsd:string): How the match was made, such as exact after cleaning, similar spelling, or confirmed by a person.
tw:matchScore (tw:NameMatch -> xsd:decimal): How alike the typed name and the matched name are, from 0 (nothing in common) to 1 (identical after cleaning).
tw:matchedTo (tw:NameMatch -> tw:Organization): Links a name match to the organization the typed name was judged to mean.
tw:mentions (tw:SupportTicket -> tw:Product): Links a support ticket to a product the customer wrote about. Found by reading the ticket text.
tw:opened (tw:Store -> xsd:date): The date a store first opened to customers.
tw:orderDate (tw:Order -> xsd:date): The day an order was placed.
tw:parentOrganization (tw:Organization -> tw:Organization): Links a company to the company that owns it.
tw:partOf (tw:Place -> tw:Place) [transitive]: Links a place to a larger place that contains it. A town in a state in a region is part of that region too.
tw:placedBy (tw:Order -> tw:Customer): Links an order to the customer who placed it.
tw:priceUSD (tw:Product -> xsd:decimal): The current shelf price of a product.
tw:problemType (tw:SupportTicket -> skos:Concept): Links a support ticket to the kind of problem it reports, from the Tarnwick problem list.
tw:product (anything -> tw:Product): Links an order line, stock level or recall to the product it is about.
tw:quantity (anything -> xsd:integer): A number of units, of a product bought on an order line or held as stock.
tw:raisedBy (tw:SupportTicket -> tw:Customer): Links a support ticket to the customer who wrote it.
tw:reason (tw:Recall -> xsd:string): Why a product was recalled, in plain words.
tw:received (tw:SupportTicket -> xsd:date): The day a support ticket arrived.
tw:releaseDate (tw:Product -> xsd:date): The first day a product could be sold.
tw:remedy (tw:Recall -> xsd:string): What customers are offered for a recalled product.
tw:replacedBy (tw:Product -> tw:Product) [inverse of tw:replaces]: Links a discontinued product to the product sold in its place.
tw:replaces (tw:Product -> tw:Product) [inverse of tw:replacedBy]: Links a product to the older product it took the place of.
tw:returnWindowDays (tw:ReturnRule -> xsd:integer): How many days after delivery a customer has to return a product under a return rule.
tw:seasonRating (tw:Product -> xsd:integer): How many seasons of the year a tent is designed for, from 2 (summer) to 4 (winter).
tw:sku (tw:Product -> xsd:string) [one value at most]: The stock keeping unit code Tarnwick gives a product, such as TW-T102.
tw:soldAt (tw:Order -> tw:Store): Links an order to the store that took it.
tw:suppliedBy (tw:Product -> tw:Supplier) [inverse of tw:supplies]: Links a product to the supplier Tarnwick buys it from.
tw:supplierNameAsTyped (tw:Product -> xsd:string): The supplier name exactly as a buyer typed it in the product file. Kept so that every supplier link can be traced back to what was written.
tw:supplies (tw:Supplier -> tw:Product) [inverse of tw:suppliedBy]: Links a supplier to a product Tarnwick buys from it.
tw:text (anything -> xsd:string): The words of a document or message, unchanged.
tw:typedName (tw:NameMatch -> xsd:string): The name exactly as it appeared in the source file.
tw:unitPriceUSD (tw:OrderLine -> xsd:decimal): The price paid for one unit on an order line.
tw:webCategoryLabel (tw:Product -> xsd:string): The category word the website shows for a product. Chosen by the web team, not taken from the product taxonomy.
tw:weightGrams (tw:Product -> xsd:integer): The weight of one unit as packed for a trip, without retail packaging. Footwear is weighed as a pair.

How the data is written:
Every thing has its name in rdfs:label, except categories and problem types, which are skos:Concept with skos:prefLabel, skos:altLabel for other names, skos:notation for the code, and skos:broader pointing to the parent category. Category labels are tagged @en.
Product statuses are tw:Active, tw:Discontinued and tw:Recalled.
Categories are in the scheme tid:categories, problem types in tid:problems.
Identifiers: products prod:TW-T102, categories cat:TENT, places place:BEND (towns in capitals), stores store:ST-BND, suppliers sup:SUP-01, customers cust:C-0001, orders order:O-00001.
Each source is loaded into its own named graph. In the default graph, each named graph has dcterms:source pointing to a thing whose rdfs:label is the file's path, or prov:wasGeneratedBy pointing to a program step whose rdfs:label names it.
The graph also holds facts a reasoner inferred: inverses, transitive partOf links, and the classes implied by subclasses.
