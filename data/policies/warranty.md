# Tarnwick Supply Warranty Policy

Policy POL-WTY, version 3, effective 1 March 2025.

Tents, packs, jackets and footwear are covered against defects in materials
and workmanship for 2 years from the date of purchase.

Stoves and cookware are covered for 1 year. Sleeping bags and sleeping mats
are covered for 1 year.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage from accidents, improper care,
or alterations. Seam leaks in a tent's first year are treated as a defect and
repaired or replaced at no cost.

To make a claim, bring the product to any Tarnwick store or contact customer
support with the order number. Tarnwick decides whether to repair, replace or
refund.
