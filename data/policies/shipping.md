# Tarnwick Supply Shipping Policy

Policy POL-SHP, version 5, effective 15 June 2025.

Tarnwick Online ships to all fifty US states. Orders over 75 dollars ship
free. Orders under 75 dollars ship for a flat 8 dollars.

Items in stock leave the warehouse within 2 business days. Delivery usually
takes 3 to 6 business days after that. Family and cabin tents ship by freight
and can take up to 10 business days.

If an order arrives damaged, contact customer support within 7 days with
photos of the box and the product. Tarnwick will send a replacement or issue
a refund.
