# Tarnwick Supply Product Recall Policy

Policy POL-RCL, version 2, effective 1 January 2026.

When Tarnwick issues a recall notice for a product, every customer who bought
that product is contacted using the details on their order.

A recalled product can be returned at any time, in any condition, with or
without proof of purchase. The customer chooses between a full refund of the
price paid and a replacement product of equal or greater value. Return
shipping is paid by Tarnwick.

Recalled products must not be resold, repaired or donated by store staff.
Stores hold them in the returns area until the supplier collects them.

The current recall notices are listed on the product safety page of the
Tarnwick Online website and posted at the service desk of every store.
