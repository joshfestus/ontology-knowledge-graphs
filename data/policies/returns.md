# Tarnwick Supply Returns Policy

Policy POL-RET, version 4, effective 1 March 2025.

Most products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused and in their original packaging.

Footwear can be returned within 60 days of delivery if it has only been worn
indoors. Boots and shoes with outdoor wear are handled under the Warranty
Policy instead.

Sleeping bags and sleeping mats can be returned within 30 days only if the
compression sack or packaging seal is unbroken, for hygiene reasons.

Products named in a recall notice are not covered by the time limits in this
policy. See the Product Recall Policy.

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 business days of
the return arriving at our warehouse. Orders placed at Tarnwick Online can be
returned to any Tarnwick store.
